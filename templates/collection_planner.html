Planning logic

Rule-based

Highest-fill simulated bins are prioritized first; this route does not use measured location-level waste data.

Critical bins are treated as immediate dispatch items, warning bins are scheduled for the next shift, and normal bins follow the standard operating plan.

Operational note

Demo

This planner is intentionally simulation-based: it does not claim live GPS routing or IoT-based optimization.

Use it as a practical operational guide while the system grows toward sensor-fed automation.
